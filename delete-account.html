Shiftly Account Deletion Request

Users can request deletion of their Shiftly account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email: support@shiftlytech.com
  2. Subject line: Delete My Shiftly Account
  3. Include the email address associated with your Shiftly account

Once we receive the request we will:

  • Delete the user account
  • Remove personal profile information
  • Remove stored preferences and account data

Some information may be retained for legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.

If you have questions contact: support@shiftlytech.com
