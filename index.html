Shiftly Privacy Policy

Shiftly respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is collected and used in the Shiftly mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account identifiers used for login and app functionality
  • Optional location data to show nearby places and live music
  • User-generated content such as favorites, pins, and posts
  • Search history used only within the app
  • Crash diagnostics used to improve stability (not linked to identity)

How We Use Information

Information is used solely to operate and improve the app, personalize the experience, and provide requested features. Shiftly does not sell personal data.

Tracking

Shiftly does not track users across apps or websites and does not use data for advertising tracking.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: support@shiftlyhub.com
